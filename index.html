Your deck is simulated at full card level — real shuffles, mulligans, prizes, search and attachment. Opponents are archetype models: Dragapult ex and N's Zoroark ex are card-exact, the rest are calibrated approximations and flagged accordingly.

Trust check: the bundled control shell scores ~53% through this engine. If a change ever drops that far from 50%, the opponent model is broken and the other numbers mean nothing — npm test enforces this.
